# CartMates Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free community. Participate with empathy,
assume good intent while addressing impact, welcome questions, give actionable feedback, respect
privacy, and disagree about ideas without attacking people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing
private information, or sustained disruption are unacceptable in project spaces. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, reject contributions, warn participants, or temporarily or permanently ban
participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled promptly,
confidentially where practical, and without retaliation. Maintainers must disclose conflicts and
recuse themselves when appropriate.
